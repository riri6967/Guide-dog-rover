            Built from your project
            • Rover drawn from the rover_description URDF meshes (chassis, wheels, encoders, RPLIDAR, Pi camera, IMU, handle and the four vibration modules), placed at the URDF joint origins.
            • Physics uses the URDF sizes: 0.27 × 0.33 m footprint, LiDAR at 0.23 m (it sees table and chair legs, not tabletops), camera at 0.10 m, grip 0.52 m behind and 0.77 m up. Speed limit 0.3 m/s from the Nav2 setup.
            • House layout and the bed, table and chair in each room come from baseline_20261010/world/assistive_world.sdf; the rest of the furniture, the laboratory and the hospital are added for these scenarios.
            • "Original firmware" cues are _cane_later/haptics.cpp line for line. "Adaptive cues" add warnings before turns, doorway, slow-down, passing and waiting signals, and a turn-around sweep. These are proposals, not existing firmware.
            • The person's trust and stress model is a design aid built from guide-dog and cane-training practice, not measured data.
