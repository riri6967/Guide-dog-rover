    Built from your project

    • House layout, walls, door posts and furniture from simulation/assistive_robot/baseline_20261010/world/assistive_world.sdf, to scale.
    • Rover footprint 0.90 × 0.76 m, speed up to 0.3 m/s, turn up to 1.0 rad/s, goal tolerance 0.15 m, from nav2_params.yaml.
    • LiDAR: 360 beams, 0.12 to 15 m; new obstacles are marked within 3.5 m, as in the tuned costmap.
    • Grip feedback ported line for line from haptic_handle/haptics.cpp: duty levels 70 / 150 / 255, deadbands 0.03 m/s and 0.10 rad/s, stop at 5 Hz, link-lost triple pulse every 1.6 s.
    • Encoder ticks: 32 per wheel turn, the slotted disc in rover_microros.ino.

    Simplified here: the planner is A* on a 10 cm grid with a pure-pursuit follower instead of SmacPlanner2D + MPPI, the rover knows its true position (no AMCL drift), and the DRL policy is not included. Treat the times as illustrative, not as new measurements.
